# Escalation Policy

## When to escalate
A finding in breach must be escalated to the security operations lead on duty.

## Approval requirement
Escalation is an irreversible action: it creates an auditable record and notifies a human. The agent
must request human approval before escalating, and must record the approver's decision.

## Environment nuance
Findings in the `dev` environment are never escalated. Open a ticket instead and note the exemption.
